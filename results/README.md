Only subset_shuffle and time_window_shuffle enter the noisy macro; CLEAN is separate. The analysis uses 100,000 shared paired bootstrap draws and a declared family of 35 simultaneous comparisons. The evaluation scope was revised after the historical results were available, and no independent confirmation is claimed.

CLEAN negative results and unresolved solver comparisons remain in the report. Previous-scope raw data, reports and figures are retained in the local archive and are absent from current release files.

[Fresh reproduced bank and separate public/evaluator traces](two_noise_families/reproduced_run/) are included. The full rerun passed in an independent directory: 10,800 trajectories, 194,400 deterministic-field comparisons, and 12 reconstructed CSVs. The physical-trace checker independently replayed all 345,526 relocation steps.
